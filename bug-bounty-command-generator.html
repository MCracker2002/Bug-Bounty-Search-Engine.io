Essential Security Practices
  • Always respect the program's scope and rules of engagement
  • Never test out-of-scope assets without explicit permission
  • Avoid destructive testing that could impact service availability
  • Do not access or modify other users' data without permission
  • Report vulnerabilities responsibly through proper channels
  • Keep your findings confidential until they are resolved
  • Use dedicated testing accounts when provided
  • Follow responsible disclosure timelines
