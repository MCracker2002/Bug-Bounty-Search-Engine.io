About Google Dorking
Google Dorking is a technique that uses advanced search operators to find specific information through Google search. In bug bounty hunting, it's used for reconnaissance and identifying potential attack surfaces.

Search Operators Used:
• site: Restricts search to specific domain
• inurl: Finds specific strings in URLs
• filetype: Searches for specific file types
• intext: Searches for text within page content
• intitle: Searches within page titles
